JEV DEMO COMMANDS
Run: uv run jev-remote --profile demo     (every command starts on Google)
Pre-warm with one throwaway command first, e.g. "search for weather".
Nothing here needs a login or a payment, and none of it buys anything.

--- LONG-RUNNING (many steps, shows the loop) ---

1. Flights, filled in by hand (shows the whole form, not Google's shortcut)
   Go to Google Flights and set a one way flight from Melbourne Florida to San Diego
   leaving this Thursday, then show the results

2. Tubi
   go to tubi tv, search duck dynasty, and select season 1 episode 4

3. Recipe to grocery list
   Find a chicken tikka masala recipe on Serious Eats and open the page with the ingredient list

4. Maps with a route
   Open Google Maps and get driving directions from Orlando to Miami, then show the route options

5. Wikipedia rabbit hole
   Open the Wikipedia page for the Apollo 11 mission, then open the page for the person who
   flew the command module

6. Weather across two steps
   Search for the 10 day forecast for San Diego and open the hourly view for tomorrow

7. Product comparison (stops before any purchase)
   Search for the best rated noise cancelling headphones under 300 dollars and open the
   top review page

8. Hotel search
   Go to Google Travel hotels, search hotels in San Diego for this weekend, and sort by lowest price

--- QUICK (under 5 seconds, good for opening the demo) ---

9. Search for how tall is the Eiffel Tower
10. Open the weather for Melbourne Florida
